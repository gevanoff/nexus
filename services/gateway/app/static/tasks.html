Five fields in UTC: minute hour day of month month weekday.
Ranges: minute 0-59, hour 0-23, day 1-31, month 1-12, weekday 0-6 where 0 is Sunday.
Use * for any value, comma lists like 1,3,5, ranges like 1-5, and steps like */15.
Examples: 0 9 * * 1-5 = 09:00 UTC on weekdays. */15 * * * * = every 15 minutes.
